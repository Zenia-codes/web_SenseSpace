An interactive, animated invitation for a children's party that includes an RSVP feature. It is fully responsive, ensuring a seamless viewing experience on mobile phones, tablets, and desktop computers. Cheerful rainbow colors paired with the "Rum Raisin" font give the invitation a playful, fairytale feel, while the clean, highly readable "Quicksand" font ensures the text is easy to read. The invitation also includes a section with gift ideas for the birthday girl. It is available in two language versions—Czech and English—with language switching handled via custom localization using JSON files rather than automatic translation.
