About me

Graphic design has been a long-term part of my work. I create flyers, posters, invitations, business cards, and logos — always with an emphasis on simplicity and meaning. Working as a print specialist has brought me closer to materials, their textures, and the physical form of the final result. This is where I developed a strong sense for color, detail, and quality.

I love technology, which is why I’m increasingly drawn to the world of front-end development. I am currently expanding my skills in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. I create visual designs in Adobe Illustrator and Photoshop, and gradually transitioning to Figma. Through my work, I increasingly realize that code and design speak the same language — just written in different scripts.

I’m drawn to simplicity and the quiet of space. Japanese minimalism resonates with me through its restraint — nothing extra, only what truly matters. In my work, I seek the same balance — lightness, calm, and space that allows things to stand out.

I am curious, detail-oriented, and open-minded. I enjoy learning new things and connecting the worlds of art and technology. I find balance in movement — especially hiking and cycling — and in the quiet of the mountains. I strive for harmony between body and mind, with respect for nature and the space around us.

Whatever I do, I give it my full attention — for myself, my children, my friends, and my clients. Simply for everyone who entrusts me with their idea.

Project

Why the name SenseSpace?

The universe is a source of inspiration for me. I imagine it as a space of infinite possibilities, where freedom naturally blends with firm laws, and where deeper meaning emerges.

This is where I create, experiment, and share thoughts, projects, and small discoveries that hold significance for me. Over time, I hope to offer practical ideas and visual solutions here, each carrying intention and value.

This website is a living project. I gradually develop it based on what I learn and what makes sense to me. I would greatly appreciate any feedback.
