About me

Graphic design has been a natural part of my work for many years. I create visual outputs such as flyers, posters, invitations, business cards and logos — always with a focus on clarity, simplicity and meaning.

As a print specialist, I developed a close relationship with materials. This experience shaped my sensitivity to color, detail and the overall quality of the final result.

I am drawn to simplicity and the calm of a space that allows what truly matters to stand out. I try to carry the same principle into my work.

When I spend time in nature, I am most drawn to textures such as wood and stone. Among man-made materials, I am particularly interested in canvas and metal.

My interest in technology naturally led me to front-end development. I am currently expanding my skills in HTML, CSS and JavaScript, while also exploring the possibilities of AI and integrating it into my creative process.

I work with tools such as Adobe Illustrator and Photoshop. I am gradually transitioning to Figma to better connect design and development workflows.

I enjoy connecting art and technology — bringing ideas to life through drawing, transforming bitmap images into vectors, and refining visuals through subtle retouching.

I believe that code and design speak the same language, just expressed in different forms.

My inspiration comes from both the digital and physical worlds — from cultural events and the environment that surrounds me daily.

Whatever I create, I give it my full attention — so that every detail has its meaning and the result appears balanced and complete.

Project

Why the name SenseSpace?

The universe is a source of inspiration for me. I imagine it as a space of infinite possibilities, where freedom naturally blends with firm laws, and where deeper meaning emerges.

This is where I create, experiment, and share thoughts, projects, and small discoveries that hold significance for me. Over time, I hope to offer practical ideas and visual solutions here.

This website is a living project. I gradually develop it based on what I learn and what makes sense to me. I would greatly appreciate any feedback.
