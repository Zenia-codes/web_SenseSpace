[ About me ]

Graphic design has been part of my work for many years. I create visual materials such as flyers, posters, invitations, business cards and logos. Thanks to my experience in print production, I have developed a strong sense of detail - colors, materials and the overall quality of the final result.

I believe that simplicity and calm in design help highlight both content and function. I focus on clear and functional user experience, complemented by an aesthetic, subtle, and visually pleasant design.

My interest in technology led me to front-end development. I am currently expanding my skills in HTML, CSS and JavaScript, while exploring the possibilities of artificial intelligence and its integration into my creative process. I code in Visual Studio Code, design in Adobe Illustrator, Photoshop and Figma.

I enjoy connecting art and technology — digital drawing, editing bitmap and vector graphics, photo retouching, creating print-ready assets, plotter cutting, and working with AI to generate image and video prompts.

I would be happy to help you transform your ideas into clear and functional digital products that are understandable, visually appealing, and easy to use.

[ Project ]

Why the name SenseSpace?

The universe is a source of inspiration for me. I see it as a space of infinite possibilities, deep meaning and freedom that naturally mixes with fixed laws. Here I want to create, experiment and share projects that are meaningful to me.

This project really helped me gain hands-on experience with JavaScript, DOM manipulation, event listeners, interactive components, modal windows, sliders, and responsive layouts using HTML/CSS Grid. It also improved my ability to debug code and solve common front-end development challenges.

This is a living project. I gradually develop it based on what I learn and what makes sense to me. I would greatly appreciate any feedback.
